# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, provide constructive feedback, and focus on
the technical issue rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing private
information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
